Verses 1–7 Exam Required

These opening verses describe the three categories of people: the Believers, the Disbelievers, and (in continuation) the Hypocrites.

1
Alif-Laam-Meem
الٓمٓ ۚ
Alif. Laam. Meem. (Disjointed letters whose true meanings are known only to Allah and His Messenger ﷺ.)
2
The Book of Guidance
ذٰلِكَ الْكِتٰبُ لَا رَيْبَ ۛ فِيْهِ ۛ هُدًى لِّلْمُتَّقِيْنَ ۙ
This is the Book in which there is no doubt whatsoever — it is a source of guidance for the God-conscious (Muttaqeen).
3
Believers' Qualities (Part 1)
الَّذِيْنَ يُؤْمِنُوْنَ بِالْغَيْبِ وَيُقِيْمُوْنَ الصَّلٰوةَ وَمِمَّا رَزَقْنٰهُمْ يُنْفِقُوْنَ ۙ
Those who believe in the Unseen (Allah, angels, revelation, the Hereafter), and establish regular prayer, and spend out of what We have provided for them (in the way of Allah).
4
Believers' Qualities (Part 2)
وَالَّذِيْنَ يُؤْمِنُوْنَ بِمَاۤ اُنْزِلَ اِلَيْكَ وَمَاۤ اُنْزِلَ مِنْ قَبْلِكَ ۚ وَبِالْاٰخِرَةِ هُمْ يُوْقِنُوْنَ ؕ
And those who believe in what has been revealed to you (O Prophet ﷺ) and in what was revealed before you, and they have firm certainty in the Hereafter.
5
The Reward
اُولٰٓئِكَ عَلٰى هُدًى مِّنْ رَّبِّهِمْ وَاُولٰٓئِكَ هُمُ الْمُفْلِحُوْنَ
These are the ones who are upon true guidance from their Lord, and it is these who are the truly successful.
6–7
The Disbelievers
اِنَّ الَّذِيْنَ كَفَرُوْا سَوَآءٌ عَلَيْهِمْ ءَاَنْذَرْتَهُمْ اَمْ لَمْ تُنْذِرْهُمْ لَا يُؤْمِنُوْنَ ۝ خَتَمَ اللّٰهُ عَلٰى قُلُوْبِهِمْ وَعَلٰى سَمْعِهِمْ ؕ وَعَلٰىۤ اَبْصَارِهِمْ غِشَاوَةٌ ۫ وَّلَهُمْ عَذَابٌ عَظِيْمٌ ۟
Indeed, those who have disbelieved — it is all the same to them whether you warn them or do not warn them — they will not believe. Allah has sealed their hearts and their hearing, and over their eyes is a covering, and for them is a great punishment.

Verse 255 — Ayat al-Kursi Exam Required

🌟 The Chief of All Verses (سیدة الآیات)

Ayat al-Kursi (The Throne Verse) is the most virtuous verse in the entire Qur'an. It comprehensively describes Allah's Oneness (Tawheed), His attributes, His sovereignty over the heavens and earth, and His absolute knowledge. Reciting it after every prayer and before sleep is a Sunnah of great blessing.

255
The Throne Verse — Complete
اَللّٰهُ لَاۤ اِلٰهَ اِلَّا هُوَ ۚ اَلْحَيُّ الْقَيُّوْمُ ۬ۚ لَا تَاْخُذُهٗ سِنَةٌ وَّلَا نَوْمٌ ؕ لَهٗ مَا فِي السَّمٰوٰتِ وَمَا فِي الْاَرْضِ ؕ مَنْ ذَا الَّذِيْ يَشْفَعُ عِنْدَهٗۤ اِلَّا بِاِذْنِهٖ ؕ يَعْلَمُ مَا بَيْنَ اَيْدِيْهِمْ وَمَا خَلْفَهُمْ ۚ وَلَا يُحِيْطُوْنَ بِشَيْءٍ مِّنْ عِلْمِهٖۤ اِلَّا بِمَا شَآءَ ۚ وَسِعَ كُرْسِيُّهُ السَّمٰوٰتِ وَالْاَرْضَ ۚ وَلَا يَئُوْدُهٗ حِفْظُهُمَا ۚ وَهُوَ الْعَلِيُّ الْعَظِيْمُ ۝۲۵۵
Allah — there is no deity worthy of worship except Him, the Ever-Living, the Sustainer of all existence. Neither drowsiness overtakes Him nor sleep. To Him belongs whatever is in the heavens and whatever is on the earth. Who is it that can intercede with Him except by His permission? He knows what is before them and what will be after them, and they encompass nothing of His knowledge except what He wills. His Kursi (Throne of Authority) extends over the heavens and the earth, and their preservation does not tire Him. And He is the Most High, the Most Great.

مَنْ ذَا الَّذِيْ يَشْفَعُ عِنْدَهٗۤ اِلَّا بِاِذْنِهٖ ؕ
Who is it that can intercede with Him except by His permission?

وَلَا يُحِيْطُوْنَ بِشَيْءٍ مِّنْ عِلْمِهٖۤ اِلَّا بِمَا شَآءَ ۚ
And they encompass nothing of His knowledge except what He wills.
وَسِعَ كُرْسِيُّهُ السَّمٰوٰتِ وَالْاَرْضَ ۚ

وَلَا يَئُوْدُهٗ حِفْظُهُمَا ۚ وَهُوَ الْعَلِيُّ الْعَظِيْمُ
And their preservation does not tire Him, and He is the Most High, the Most Great.

Verse 275 — Prohibition of Riba (Interest) Exam Required

⚠️ The Grave Warning Against Interest

This verse explicitly forbids Riba (usury/interest), comparing those who consume it to individuals possessed by Satan. It sharply distinguishes lawful trade from unlawful interest, calling Riba a cause of eternal punishment for the persistent offender.

275
The Consumers of Riba
اَلَّذِيْنَ يَاْكُلُوْنَ الرِّبٰوا لَا يَقُوْمُوْنَ اِلَّا كَمَا يَقُوْمُ الَّذِيْ يَتَخَبَّطُهُ الشَّيْطٰنُ مِنَ الْمَسِّ ؕ ذٰلِكَ بِاَنَّهُمْ قَالُوْۤا اِنَّمَا الْبَيْعُ مِثْلُ الرِّبٰوا ۘ وَاَحَلَّ اللّٰهُ الْبَيْعَ وَحَرَّمَ الرِّبٰوا ؕ فَمَنْ جَآءَهٗ مَوْعِظَةٌ مِّنْ رَّبِّهٖ فَانْتَهٰى فَلَهٗ مَا سَلَفَ ؕ وَاَمْرُهٗۤ اِلَى اللّٰهِ ؕ وَمَنْ عَادَ فَاُولٰٓئِكَ اَصْحٰبُ النَّارِ ۚ هُمْ فِيْهَا خٰلِدُوْنَ ۝۲۷۵
Those who consume interest (Riba) will not stand (on the Day of Judgment) except as one stands who is being tormented by Satan's touch. That is because they say, "Trade is just like interest." But Allah has permitted trade and forbidden interest. So whoever receives an admonition from his Lord and desists — he may keep what he took previously, and his affair rests with Allah. But whoever returns to (consuming interest) — those are the companions of the Fire; they will abide therein eternally.

Segment Breakdown

اَلَّذِيْنَ يَاْكُلُوْنَ الرِّبٰوا لَا يَقُوْمُوْنَ اِلَّا كَمَا يَقُوْمُ الَّذِيْ يَتَخَبَّطُهُ الشَّيْطٰنُ مِنَ الْمَسِّ ؕ
Those who consume interest will not stand (on the Day of Judgment) except as one stands who has been driven mad by Satan's touch.
ذٰلِكَ بِاَنَّهُمْ قَالُوْۤا اِنَّمَا الْبَيْعُ مِثْلُ الرِّبٰوا ۘ
That is because they say, "Trade is just like interest."
وَاَحَلَّ اللّٰهُ الْبَيْعَ وَحَرَّمَ الرِّبٰوا ؕ
But Allah has permitted trade and forbidden interest.
فَمَنْ جَآءَهٗ مَوْعِظَةٌ مِّنْ رَّبِّهٖ فَانْتَهٰى فَلَهٗ مَا سَلَفَ ؕ وَاَمْرُهٗۤ اِلَى اللّٰهِ ؕ
So whoever receives admonition from his Lord and desists — what came before is his (to keep), and his affair rests with Allah.
وَمَنْ عَادَ فَاُولٰٓئِكَ اَصْحٰبُ النَّارِ ۚ هُمْ فِيْهَا خٰلِدُوْنَ ۝۲۷۵
And whoever returns (to consuming interest) — those are the companions of the Fire; they will abide therein eternally.

Verses 284–286 — The Closing Supplication Exam Required

🕌 The Final Two Verses of Baqarah

These verses close Surah Al-Baqarah with a magnificent summary: Allah's sovereignty, the affirmation of universal faith, and one of the most beloved supplications in the entire Qur'an. The Prophet ﷺ said: "Whoever recites the last two verses of Surah Al-Baqarah at night, they will be sufficient for him" (Sahih Bukhari: 5009).

284
Allah's Sovereignty & Accountability
لِلّٰهِ مَا فِي السَّمٰوٰتِ وَمَا فِي الْاَرْضِ ؕ وَاِنْ تُبْدُوْا مَا فِيْۤ اَنْفُسِكُمْ اَوْ تُخْفُوْهُ يُحَاسِبْكُمْ بِهِ اللّٰهُ ؕ فَيَغْفِرُ لِمَنْ يَّشَآءُ وَيُعَذِّبُ مَنْ يَّشَآءُ ؕ وَاللّٰهُ عَلٰى كُلِّ شَيْءٍ قَدِيْرٌ ۝۲۸۴
To Allah belongs whatever is in the heavens and whatever is on the earth. And whether you disclose what is within yourselves or conceal it, Allah will bring you to account for it. Then He will forgive whom He wills and punish whom He wills. And Allah is Powerful over all things.
285
Universal Faith Affirmation (Aamana ar-Rasul)
اٰمَنَ الرَّسُوْلُ بِمَاۤ اُنْزِلَ اِلَيْهِ مِنْ رَّبِّهٖ وَالْمُؤْمِنُوْنَ ؕ كُلٌّ اٰمَنَ بِاللّٰهِ وَمَلٰٓئِكَتِهٖ وَكُتُبِهٖ وَرُسُلِهٖ ۟ لَا نُفَرِّقُ بَيْنَ اَحَدٍ مِّنْ رُسُلِهٖ ۟ وَقَالُوْا سَمِعْنَا وَاَطَعْنَا ٭ؗ غُفْرَانَكَ رَبَّنَا وَاِلَيْكَ الْمَصِيْرُ ۝۲۸۵
The Messenger (ﷺ) has believed in what was revealed to him from his Lord, and (so have) the believers. All of them have believed in Allah, His angels, His books, and His messengers, (saying): "We make no distinction between any of His messengers." And they said: "We hear and we obey. (We seek) Your forgiveness, our Lord, and to You is the (final) destination."
286
The Great Supplication
لَا يُكَلِّفُ اللّٰهُ نَفْسًا اِلَّا وُسْعَهَا ؕ لَهَا مَا كَسَبَتْ وَعَلَيْهَا مَا اكْتَسَبَتْ ؕ رَبَّنَا لَا تُؤَاخِذْنَاۤ اِنْ نَّسِيْنَاۤ اَوْ اَخْطَاْنَا ۚ رَبَّنَا وَلَا تَحْمِلْ عَلَيْنَاۤ اِصْرًا كَمَا حَمَلْتَهٗ عَلَى الَّذِيْنَ مِنْ قَبْلِنَا ۚ رَبَّنَا وَلَا تُحَمِّلْنَا مَا لَا طَاقَةَ لَنَا بِهٖ ۚ وَاعْفُ عَنَّا ۪ وَاغْفِرْ لَنَا ۪ وَارْحَمْنَا ۪ اَنْتَ مَوْلٰىنَا فَانْصُرْنَا عَلَى الْقَوْمِ الْكٰفِرِيْنَ ۝۲۸۶
Allah does not burden a soul beyond its capacity. For it is (the reward of) what it has earned, and against it is (the punishment of) what it has earned (of evil). "Our Lord! Do not take us to task if we forget or make a mistake. Our Lord! Do not lay upon us a burden like that which You laid upon those before us. Our Lord! Do not burden us with more than we have the strength to bear. Pardon us, forgive us, and have mercy upon us. You are our Protector, so grant us victory over the disbelieving people."

Verse 286 — Segment Breakdown for Memorization

Allah does not burden a soul beyond its capacity.
